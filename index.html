About me

Assalam-o-Alaikum! I'm Hamna, a Software Engineer and Lecturer from Karachi, Pakistan.

I'm a Junior Lecturer at IoBM (Institute of Business Management), where I teach Data Structures and Software Engineering courses. Before that I was a Faculty Member at Aptech, teaching Web Development and Programming.

I hold a B.S. in Software Engineering from Jinnah University for Women and I'm now pursuing my Master's at NED University.

Alongside teaching, I build websites and web apps for clients, and I'm an experienced event manager and organizer.
