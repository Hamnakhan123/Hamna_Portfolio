About me

Assalam-o-Alaikum! I'm Hamna, a Junior Lecturer and Software Engineer from Karachi, Pakistan, and a proud Sagittarius: curious, optimistic and always aiming higher.

I'm a Junior Lecturer at IoBM (Institute of Business Management), where I teach Data Structures, Programming Fundamentals and ICT labs as a Lecturer and Lab Engineer. Before that I was a Faculty Member at Aptech, teaching Web Development and Programming.

I hold a B.S. in Software Engineering from Jinnah University for Women and I'm now pursuing my Master's at NED University.

Alongside teaching, I build modern web apps with React, Node and Firebase, mentor students, and I'm an experienced event manager and organizer.
